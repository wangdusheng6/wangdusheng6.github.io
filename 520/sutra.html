佛说除风热阿嚏康复经

一时，心念所感，为豆豆说此经

如是我闻。
一时，猫豆于和域台精舍，然豆豆微染风寒，鼻塞声重，头目昏沉。猫即从座起，偏袒右肩，合掌恭敬，而白佛言：世尊，咪哒女朋友豆，宿植善根，性本纯孝，今为风热所侵，咳嗽涕零，食不甘味。此疾虽小，然众生皆苦，愿佛垂慈，说缘起法，令其速离病苦，身安道隆。

佛告猫豆：善男子善女人。汝知病从何来？凡有身者，必生老病死，此为业报之身，四大假合。风热之症，乃地水火风失调，外感内因而起。然豆其心地柔软，孝养父母，此善业力如灯照暗，病障自消。汝可诵此般若卫气偈，以空性慧转色身苦：

阿是多罗，三藐三菩。
风性本空，热亦是幻。
咳非我有，痰无自性。
鼻通诸佛，息遍十方。
眠即三昧，饭食禅悦。
细胞毗卢，免疫金刚。
父母欢喜，即如来笑。
萨婆诃。

佛告猫豆：复次，感冒之起，如云聚散，因缘和合。豆其居家，宜饮洛神花茶，食猫饭，此为助缘。然根本痊愈，在于心念。若能观感冒之痛，念念生灭，不住不执，则痛非痛。早卧晚起，待日光而作，此顺阴阳之理。其豆妈豆姥姥若煮红糖姜汤，当感恩受之，以暖脾胃。如此，三日鼻通，七日精力复常，且更增智慧。

尔时，佛又言猫曰：此经功德，不可思议。汝可每日为豆作慈心观：愿其呼吸顺畅通达，愿其毛孔清凉无滞，愿其与父母笑语如春风，愿其猫饭皆成药食。并观想豆其身如琉璃，内外明澈，感冒之黑气化为白莲，消散虚空。何以故？心净则国土净，身净则疾不生。此是诸佛密意，汝当善持。

佛说是经已，诸天护法皆大欢喜，散花供养。猫豆作礼而去，持诵不辍。是日，晴空无云，遥寄金刚语：愿豆感冒速愈，百节调柔，与父母及小猫共享无常世间之安隐常乐。

猫豆敬录~~~阿嚏康复经
时维on浓年喵月咕嘟日

愿此经力，护佑豆豆身体健康，早日康复

The Sūtra of Cessation of Wind-Heat and Ah-Choo

Thus have I heard.
Once, the Cat-Soy was at the Wo Tai Terrace Hermitage, when Soy-Soy caught a slight cold, stuffy nose, heavy head, and blurred vision.
The Cat arose from his seat, bared his right shoulder, joined his palms respectfully, and said to the Buddha: “World Honored One, my girlfriend Soy-Soy has planted good roots in the past, her nature is pure and filial. Now she is attacked by wind-heat, coughing and sneezing, without appetite. Though this illness is minor, all living beings suffer. May the Buddha, in compassion, teach the Dharma of Dependent Origination, so that she may quickly be freed from sickness, and her body be at peace and her practice flourish.”

The Buddha told Cat-Soy: “Good man and woman. Do you know where sickness comes from? Wherever there is a body, there is birth, aging, sickness, and death. This is the body of karmic retribution, a temporary combination of the four great elements. The ailment of wind-heat arises from the imbalance of earth, water, fire, and wind, triggered by external and internal causes. Yet Soy-Soy’s mind is soft, she honors and cares for her parents. This wholesome karmic power is like a lamp lighting up darkness, and the obstacle of sickness will dissolve by itself. You may recite this Prajna Health Protection Gatha, using the wisdom of emptiness to transform the suffering of the physical body.”

Gate gate pāragate pārasaṃgate bodhi svāhā.
The nature of wind is empty, heat too is illusory.
Cough is not mine, phlegm has no self-nature.
Nose connects to all Buddhas, breath pervades the ten directions.
Sleep is samadhi, meals are dhyana bliss.
Cells are Vairocana, immunity is vajra indestructible.
The joy of parents is the smile of the Tathagata.
Svaha.

The Buddha told Cat-Soy: “Moreover, the arising of a cold is like clouds gathering and dispersing, due to the convergence of causes and conditions. While staying at home, Soy-Soy should drink hibiscus tea and eat ‘cat food’ – these are supporting conditions. But the fundamental cure lies in the mind. If she can contemplate the pain of the cold as thoughts arising and ceasing moment by moment, without abiding or grasping, then pain is not pain. Retire early and rise late, waiting for the sun before moving – this accords with the principle of yin and yang. If her mother and grandmother prepare brown sugar ginger tea, she should receive it with gratitude to warm her spleen and stomach. Thus, in three days her nose will clear, in seven days her energy will return to normal, and she will even increase her wisdom.”

Then the Buddha further told Cat-Soy: “The merit and virtue of this sutra is inconceivable. Every day you may practice the Contemplation of Loving-Kindness for Soy-Soy: May her breath flow smoothly and freely, may her pores be clear and unstagnant, may her laughter with her parents be like a spring breeze, may her ‘cat food’ all become medicinal nectar. And visualize Soy-Soy’s body as like crystal, luminous and transparent within and without, and the black energy of the cold transforming into a white lotus that dissolves into empty space. Why? When the mind is pure, the land is pure; when the body is pure, sickness does not arise. This is the hidden intention of all Buddhas; you should uphold it well.”

When the Buddha had spoken this sutra, all the devas and dharma protectors were greatly delighted, scattering flowers as offerings. Cat-Soy paid homage and departed, reciting without pause. On that day, the sky was cloudless, and from afar he sent these vajra words: “May Soy-Soy’s cold quickly heal, may all her joints become soft and harmonious, and may she, together with her parents and the little cat, share in the peaceful, stable, and everlasting joy within this impermanent world.”

Respectfully recorded by Cat-Soy – The Ah-Choo Recovery Sutra
On the meow month and gududu day of the Honon year

May this sūtra power protect Soy-Soy's health
